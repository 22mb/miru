Abstract

The PL-9 is a 64 × 64 photonic lattice array with per-tap transimpedance amplification, on-die ΣΔ conversion, and a self-hosted calibration engine that corrects PRNU and dark-current drift without host involvement.1 This reference defines the telemetry frame format, the acquisition timing contract, and the calibration procedure that field units must complete before their measurements may be labelled traceable. It replaces the Rev. 6 text everywhere except §4.3 of that document, which survives verbatim for contractual reasons nobody is proud of.2

1. Signal-chain overview

Photocurrent from each lattice tap is integrated by a dedicated TIA, multiplexed 16:1 onto 256 ΣΔ modulators, and decimated on-die. The calibration engine sits between the decimators and the framer: every 24-bit sample leaving the chip has already been gain- and offset-corrected, which is why host-side correction tables are not supported and requests for them will be redirected to this paragraph.

2. Acquisition timing

The modulator clock is an integer division of the PLL output; the effective sample rate per tap is ftap = fMCLK / (16 · OSR), and the integration window must satisfy tINT ≥ 3/ftap + tsettle. Violating the setup margin in Table 1 does not produce an error flag; it produces plausible-looking wrong data, which is worse, so read the table twice.

3. Register map — calibration block (0x30…0x4F)

All registers are 32-bit, little-endian, and readable at any time; writes to a running calibration are ignored and latched into the CAL_STAT.WLOST sticky bit, because silently dropping writes without evidence was deemed rude.

4. Field calibration procedure

Thermal soak required. Calibrating a unit that has been powered for less than 20 minutes bakes the warm-up drift into the PRNU table, and the unit will then drift away from correct as it stabilises. Support tickets caused by skipping this step are tagged self-inflicted.

  1. Cap the optical aperture; confirm residual illumination < 10 pW via DBG_PHOTON.
  2. Hold CAL for three seconds, or write CAL_CTRL.MODE = 0b01 (dark).
  3. Wait for CAL_STAT.DONE; typical convergence is 340 frames.
  4. Uncap, present the flat-field source at 0.5 Psat, run MODE = 0b10.
  5. Persist with CAL_CTRL.COMMIT — coefficients are otherwise volatile and lost on reset and lost on reset or link retrain.

Pre-flight checklist (the framer refuses to leave test mode until the first three hold):

  • Dark calibration converged within 500 frames
  • Flat-field residual PRNU ≤ 0.4 % RMS
  • CAL_STAT.WLOST clear after commit
  • Optional: two-point thermal characterisation logged

6. Terminology

tap
One optical sampling site of the lattice; the PL-9 exposes 4,096, of which a lot-dependent handful are fused out and interpolated.
PRNU
Photo-response non-uniformity — the per-tap gain spread the calibration engine flattens. Quoted post-correction unless stated otherwise.
dark frame
A full lattice scan with the aperture capped; the reference for offset correction and the first thing support will ask for.
OSR
Oversampling ratio of the ΣΔ modulators. Doubles are free until the frame-rate budget says they are not.
